How It Works

Getting started only takes a few minutes.

1

Create an Account

Register with your name, phone number, and darts platform — takes under a minute.

2

Choose Your Platform

Let us know whether you play on Dartcounter, Scolia, or in person, so you're placed correctly.

3

Join a League or Tournament

Browse what's currently open and hit Join — no approval needed for Online events.

4

Arrange Your Matches

Once fixtures are set, message your opponent via WhatsApp to agree a time to play.

5

Play & Enter Your Result

Play your match, then enter the score directly on the site — it updates the standings straight away.

Get the mobile app

Add Pinnacle Darts to your home screen for quick access, just like a normal app.

📱 iPhone (Safari)
1

Open in Safari

Make sure you're viewing the site in Safari, not another browser.

2

Tap the Share icon

It's the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom toolbar.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the share menu if you don't see it straight away.

4

Tap "Add"

The app icon will appear on your home screen, ready to open like any app.

🤖 Android (Chrome)
1

Open in Chrome

Make sure you're viewing the site in Chrome.

2

Tap the menu (⋮)

It's in the top-right corner of the browser.

3

Tap "Install app"

On some devices this shows as "Add to Home screen" instead.

4

Tap "Install"

The app icon will appear on your home screen, ready to open like any app.
